## Open questions for synthesis

1. **Multi-resource cost conjunction ergonomics (Interlocker #3 vs. Scaler #6 vs. Phase-shifter dependency chains).** Interlocker #3 marks a `partial` for the multi-resource cost-requirement conjunction, citing future graduation of `createMultiResourceCost`. The synthesis pass should verify whether the conjunction-array ergonomics gap in Interlocker is distinct from the other archetypes' conjunction usages or whether all five archetypes converge on the same N=multiple-consumer signal for the helper.

2. **Offline efficiency coefficient (Ambient #6 partial) vs. processOfflineTime scope.** The `offlineEfficiency < 1.0` design knob appears in Ambient's balance struct but is not a native `processOfflineTime` option. If the Looper archetype (chunk 2) or Scaler archetype also ships an offline-efficiency knob (Scaler's prestige runs offline accumulation during idle), the convergence would promote the option. Synthesis: check whether any non-Ambient archetype in other chunks also needs a per-rate efficiency multiplier applied during offline processing.

3. **costScaling: { threshold, scale } candidate across chunks.** The AD-shaped piecewise-quadratic-in-log cost scaling (Scaler #2 partial) is already in `candidate-primitives.md` as a candidate with no engine-side consumer yet. Phase-shifter's linear cost (row 3) and Interlocker's mild geometric (row 2) both differ in shape from the AD form — they're simpler and don't need the piecewise extension. But if any chunk-2 or chunk-3 archetype also hits the piecewise-in-log shape (Looper might), that would be the N=2 signal for the candidate to graduate. Flag for synthesis.

4. **createPhaseMachine graduation threshold.** Phase-shifter rows 9 and 13 both carry `partial` verdicts citing the `createPhaseMachine` candidate (graduation trigger: first consumer writes ≥3 `Grant.when`-gated chains for the same phase axis). A mature Phase-shifter implementation with 3 phases (Paperclips P1/P2/P3 shape) will hit this threshold on first implementation. The synthesis pass should note: Phase-shifter archetype implementation is likely the graduation trigger, not a second independent consumer. This changes the graduation from "wait for N=2" to "implement Phase-shifter, graduate from N=1 sufficient evidence."

5. **Recipe primitive N-in/M-out convergence between Interlocker and Auto-battler.** The `candidate-primitives.md` "Combine-up recipe primitive" entry cites both Interlocker (OL-541) and Auto-battler (OL-578) as consumers. The Interlocker use case (N resources in → M resources out per tick, as a production recipe) and the Auto-battler use case (combine N identical items → M upgraded items, as an inventory recipe) may have different identity/scoring requirements. The synthesis pass should evaluate whether a unified primitive shape exists or whether these split into two siblings. If they unify, the graduation ticket should be filed immediately (N=2 independent consumers already documented).

6. **Scaler #4/#5 prestige conversion formula invertibility for "next at" display.** Both conversion rows adopt `createCumulativeConversion` but the AD formula (`10^(log10(maxAM)/div − 0.75)`) needs a `Formula` chain to support the `nextAt` display (invertible). `ConversionOptions.formula` accepts either a `GenericFormula` or a plain function — but only `GenericFormula` types with `invert()` support `nextAt`. Synthesis: verify that `Formula.variable(x).log10().div(div).sub(0.75)` then `Formula.pow(10, ...)` is a valid invertible chain in the engine's formula system, or whether the consumer must implement the inverse manually as a plain function and skip `nextAt`.
